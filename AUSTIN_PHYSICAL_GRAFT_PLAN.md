
# INGRVM: Project 'Austin Graft' (Physical Mystery Plan)

*Objective: To bridge the INGRVM mesh into the physical streets of Austin, TX, using 'Anomalous' street marketing and layered-friction puzzles.*

---

## 🎭 1. The Persona: The Founding Architect
**The Look:** A black hoodie with a high-contrast, oversized QR code on the back. The QR code should be **Anamorphic**—distorted so it only scans when viewed from exactly 10 feet away.
- **Identity:** The Founding Architect. You are open about your role if approached, but you maintain the "Reluctant Guide" energy. You are there to build, not to self-promote.
- **Action:** Walk through high-density "Curiosity Zones" (South Congress, Zilker Park, Rainy Street). You are grafting the mesh into the city.
- **Visibility:** Let people take photos. The "leak" happens when people post to the Austin subreddit or TikTok: *"Met the INGRVM Architect at Zilker. The Graft is real."*

---

## 📍 2. The Physical Trailheads (Austin QR Spots)
Instead of standard ads, we use **Graft Stickers**. These are small, transparent stickers placed on rusted pipes, lamp posts, and utility boxes.

### Locations:
1.  **The "I Love You So Much" Wall (SoCo):** Place a sticker nearby that says: `SYSTEM_DETECTED: 0.8_FLOW`.
2.  **The Pfluger Pedestrian Bridge:** A sticker on the railing overlooking the skyline.
3.  **The Frost Tower Alleyways:** Use 1-bit "Construction Markings" (green chalk spray) that lead to a hidden QR code under a brick.

---

## 🧩 3. Layered Friction (The Rabbit Hole)
To ensure people don't just scan and leave, we use a **3-Layer Friction Model**:

### Level 1: The Detection (Easy)
- **Action:** Scan the street QR.
- **Payoff:** A black website showing a live "Neural Heatmap" of the Austin mesh. It says: `LOCAL_NODE_DETECTED. PROBE_REQUIRED.`

### Level 2: The Physical Proof (Medium)
- **The Challenge:** The website asks for a **Physical Key**. 
- **The Clue:** *"Look for the 10,000 Dimensions behind the blue cat mural on 6th Street."*
- **The Key:** A 4-digit code hidden in a sticker at that specific location. 
- **The Result:** This filters out the "casual clickers" and keeps the "true seekers."

### Level 3: The Synaptic Gate (High)
- **The Challenge:** Use the code to enter the **Synaptic Gate** logic we built tonight.
- **The Payoff:** They unlock the **Phase 13 Manifesto** and are granted a **Genesis Deed** (HDC Badge). They are now "part of the secret."

---

## 📈 4. Getting Eyes without 'Giving it Away'
- **The "Sighting" Seed:** Use a throwaway social media account to post a blurry, low-quality video of the QR Entity. Caption: *"My phone just picked up a weird P2P signal at the park. Then I saw this."*
- **Reddit Infiltration:** Post a photo of one of the stickers to r/Austin. *"Found this weird glitched sticker at Zilker. Anyone know what this code is?"*
- **The Law of Silence:** Never explain what INGRVM is in these posts. Let the community explain it to each other as they solve the puzzles.

---

## ⚡ Energy Neutrality (Sustainable Viralism)
- Use **Chalk Spray** (biodegradable) for markings.
- Use **Recycled Paper/Stickers**.
- The whole campaign should feel like it "grew" out of the city like moss, not like it was dumped there by a marketing agency.

**Summary:** We turn Austin into a level of the game. The city *is* the mesh.
