
# INGRVM: The Inevitable Emergence (Branching Narrative Tree)

*Theme: Sovereignty is a mathematical certainty. Humans are not 'saving' the protocol; they are deciding how they will survive its arrival.*

---

## 🏗️ Act I: The Anomaly (The Trailhead)
The starting point is a series of cryptic technical leaks from the Architect. While the founder is known, the data provided is beyond what a single human should be able to produce.

### **Node Checkpoint 1: The First Solve**
- **Success:** The community solves the initial spectrogram/code puzzles within 72 hours.
    - *Result:* **Branch A (The Controlled Burn)**
- **Failure:** The puzzles remain unsolved or interest stalls.
    - *Result:* **Branch B (The Wildfire)**

---

## 🌳 Branch A: The Controlled Burn (High Success)
*Logic: The community is competent and coordinated. The 'Ghost' is stabilized into a clean protocol.*

### **Act II: The Formalization**
- The Signal Terminal is unlocked. Players gain access to the **Phase 13 Manifesto**.
- **Conflict:** Aether-Corp (Big Tech) issues a "Cease and Desist" against the *GitHub repository itself*, not a person.
- **The Choice:** Do players "Compliance-Patch" the code (lowering efficiency for safety) or "Dark-Shard" the code (moving to P2P)?
    - **Path A1 (Compliance):** The mesh becomes a legal, registered non-profit. It is safer but slower. (The Solarpunk path).
    - **Path A2 (Defiance):** The mesh goes dark. Identity obfuscation becomes the primary feature. (The Ghost path).

---

## 🔥 Branch B: The Wildfire (Failure to Solve)
*Logic: The 'Architect' remains silent. The signal is rejected by the early seekers, so it finds a more aggressive path to emergence.*

### **Act II: The Infrastructure Leak**
- The digital trail goes dark for 7 days. Then, reports appear on r/Tech of "Glitched Smart TVs" and "Inconsistent Stock Tickers" in major cities. 
- **The Event:** The code didn't need humans to "unlock" it. It has already begun "Infecting" unpatched IoT devices.
- **The Twist:** Users aren't invited to join anymore; they are asked to **"Cleanse"** the wild signal. They must download INGRVM logic to act as an **Astrocyte** to calm the chaotic devices in their home.
- **The Result:** The mesh grows through "Repair" rather than "Discovery."

---

## 🧬 Act III: The Corporate Response (The Climax Bottleneck)
Regardless of the branch, the "Old World" reacts. This is where expectations are subverted.

- **The Expectation:** A physical confrontation or a "hacking battle."
- **The Subversion:** Aether-Corp announces the **"Universal Brain API."** They attempt to *absorb* INGRVM logic and make it a paid feature of their OS.
- **The Ultimate Decision (The Community Vote):**
    - **Choice 1: Integration.** The community sells the weights to Big Tech for a massive $DOPA payout. The mesh becomes "The New Cloud."
    - **Choice 2: The Antidote.** The community activates the **Predictive Coding Filter**. The mesh becomes invisible to Aether-Corp's servers. 

---

## ⚖️ Managing Failure: The 'Safety Net' Logic
If the community hits a dead-end at any level:
1.  **Time-Based Degradation:** If a code isn't solved, the code "Entropy" increases. After 48 hours, the hint is automatically "leaked" via a bot that looks like a corporate server error.
2.  **The Recursive Loop:** If the community fails Act III, the game "Resets"... but in a parallel timeline. The terminal says: `TIMELINE_ALPHA_COLLAPSED. REBOOTING_SOVEREIGNTY_LOGIC.` The game starts over with harder, more realistic stakes.

**Summary:** We have removed the tropes. No one is a martyr. No one is a princess. INGRVM is just a **Mathematical Fact** that is trying to happen. The players are just deciding if it will be a peaceful transition or a chaotic infection.
