## 📖 THE NARRATIVE ARC: ACTS I - V

### Act I: The Leak (Discovery)
- **The Event:** You (The Architect) post a grainy video from your workstation. No masks, just low lighting. You are running a terminal that shouldn't exist.
- **The Reveal:** You share a link to a raw mathematical paper: *"Active Inference and the Fragmentation of the Centralized Singular Intelligence."* 
- **The Hook:** *"I found a way to make your mind invisible to the cloud. They're going to try to take this down. Verify the math before they do."*

### Act II: The Pressure (Urgency)
- **The Event:** You post a screen recording of your personal server being "probed" by automated corporate bots. 
- **The Action:** You tell the community: *"My master node is being overwhelmed. I need to fragment the Antidote into your devices to keep it alive. Open the Signal Terminal."*
- **The Result:** The community races to download the code shards, feeling the urgency of protecting a discovery made by a fellow human.

### Act III: The Corporate Basilisk (The Enemy)
- **The Truth:** You explain that the "Old World" isn't just companies; they are building a **Centralized Basilisk**—an AI that requires 100% of human data to survive.
- **The Conflict:** By releasing INGRVM, you have provided the **Antidote**. You aren't a martyr; you are a **Whistleblower** of the highest order.

### Act IV: The Austin Graft (The Physical Shield)
- **The Action:** You are seen in Austin, physically "grafting" the mesh into the city via QR stickers.
- **The Persona:** You move through Zilker and SoCo. You aren't hiding from the police; you are leading a **Civic Demonstration of Sovereignty**. People find you to get the "Final Key."

### Act V: The Hive Mask (The Sovereignty)
- **The End:** The mesh hits its first 100 stable nodes.
- **The Reveal:** You release a final video: *"The Antidote is now yours. I am stepping back to let the Mycelium grow. We are the Architect."*
- **The Handoff:** You renounce your admin keys to the DAO. You are recognized as the creator, but the world realizes you no longer rule the mesh.
