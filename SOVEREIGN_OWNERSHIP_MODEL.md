
# INGRVM: Sovereign Ownership & The Synaptic Registry

*How we handle Intellectual Property, Content Rights, and Hardware Ownership in a world of shared 1-bit pulses.*

---

## 🏛️ 1. The Three Layers of Ownership

In the Old World, ownership is a paper contract held by a bank or a corporation. In INGRVM, ownership is a **Mathematical Fact** etched into the blockchain and the synapses themselves.

### A. Substrate Ownership (The Hardware)
You physically own your node (phone, laptop, server). Unlike cloud computing (AWS), where you rent "space" from Amazon, INGRVM nodes are **Private Property**. 
- **The Right:** You have the absolute right to turn your node off, wipe its memory, or set its **Metabolic Gater** to zero. No one can force your hardware to process spikes against your will.

### B. Synaptic Ownership (The Weights)
When a creator trains a new AI skill (e.g., "Medical Diagnosis" or "Indie Movie Vibe"), they create a **Synapse**.
- **Synaptic Fingerprinting:** Every weight set is "Watermarked" with a unique mathematical signature. 
- **On-Chain Deeds:** This signature is registered on the INGRVM Layer 1 as a **Synaptic Deed**. 
- **The Royalty Loop:** Whenever those weights are used to process a task in the mesh, the **Reward Engine** automatically routes a percentage of the $DOPA to the owner of that Deed.

### C. Contextual Ownership (The Data)
Your personal data (the things you say to your AI) never leaves your device.
- **Sovereign Privacy:** Because of **Differential Privacy (DP)** and **Local STDP**, you own the "Changes" your node makes to its brain. 
- **The Result:** If your node becomes "Smarter" because it spent a year learning your habits, **you own that intelligence**. It cannot be "Extracted" by a corporation because it is spread across 10,000 HDC dimensions that only your node can interpret.

## 🔒 2. Preventing "Synaptic Theft"
If someone copies my weight file, don't they own my AI? 
**NO.** 

We use **Proof of Attribution (PoA)**:
1.  **Steganographic Watermarking:** We hide a secret "Trigger Spike" pattern inside the weights. 
2.  **The Challenge:** If a suspicious node claims to own a model, the mesh sends a **Challenge Spike**. 
3.  **The Response:** Only the original weights, with the secret watermark intact, will fire the correct **HDC Response Vector**. If the node fails, it is flagged as a "Parasite Node," its reputation is slashed, and its stolen weights are rejected by the mesh.

## 🎨 3. Fractional Creative Ownership
For a movie or game built on the mesh:
- **The Creators:** The Director, the Actors, and the AI Trainer all hold fractional shares of the **Synaptic Deed**.
- **The Audience:** Even the fans can own a piece! If you provide the compute power to "Host" the movie's layers, you earn a micro-share of the revenue generated by anyone watching it through your node.

**Summary:** We have moved from "Centralized Renting" to **"Distributed Equity."** 

**In INGRVM, if you provide the hardware, the data, or the creativity, you are a shareholder in the intelligence you create.**
