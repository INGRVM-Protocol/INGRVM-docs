## 🤝 1. Indirect Reciprocity (The Kindness Ledger)
**The Problem:** In a big world, you can't always trade 1-for-1. I might give you eggs, but you don't have anything I need right now.
**The INGRVM Solution:** We use **$DOPA** and **Reputation** as a buffer for kindness.
- **Mechanism:** When you perform a "Kindness Act" (e.g., repairing a neighbor's solar inverter or sharing a harvest), the neighbor sends a **Gratitude Spike** to your node.
- **Result:** This increases your **Social Reputation Score**. Later, when you need a tool from someone else in the mesh, your high Reputation proves you are a "Seeder" (contributor), making them more likely to trust and trade with you. Kindness is never "lost"; it is stored in the mesh.

## ⚡ 2. Mycelium Trusts (Trust-Based Grafts)
**The Problem:** How do you trust a stranger in a decentralized world?
**The INGRVM Solution:** We use our **Mycelium Graft** system to map physical trust.
- **Mechanism:** You only "Graft" (connect) your node to people you actually know and trust in the physical world. 
- **The "Trust Path":** If a stranger wants to trade with you, the INGRVM app shows you the **Trust Distance**. ("This person is trusted by your friend Dave"). 
- **The Shield:** If someone acts maliciously, the local "Mycelium" (the neighborhood nodes) collectively **Prunes** them. They lose their connection to the local resource mesh, making "terror" or "harm" socially and economically impossible.

## 🛠️ 3. Resource NCPs (Shared Commons)
**The Problem:** Shared resources (like a community well or a tool library) often suffer from the "Tragedy of the Commons" (some people take too much).
**The INGRVM Solution:** We deploy **NCP (Neuromorphic Context Protocol) Servers** to physical objects.
- **Smart Tool Library:** A shared lawnmower or 3D printer is equipped with an NCP server. It streams its "health spikes" to the mesh.
- **Automatic Fairness:** The INGRVM mesh monitors the usage spikes. If one person uses the tool 90% of the time without contributing back to the mesh, their **Metabolic Gater** (Level 5) automatically increases the "Cost" for them to access the tool. The system self-regulates for fairness without a human manager.
