## 🦠 1. Digital Oncology (Malware Defense)
Evil is mathematically **Chaotic**. We use this fact to protect the mesh.
- **Synaptic Auditing:** Before accepting a new synapse (weights), a node stress-tests it with high-entropy white noise.
- **The Detection:** Trojan logic "explodes" under this noise. The node identifies the instability and **rejects the synapse** as an infection.
- **Astrocyte Oversight:** Astrocytes monitor "Metabolic Spikes." If a synapse fires too hot or too fast (designed to overheat a device), the Astrocyte strangles the power.

## ⚖️ 2. Gravitational Ethics (The Rulers)
We don't use "Admins." We use **Mathematical Gravity**.
- **Quadratic Reputation:** Voting power is the **Square Root of Reputation**. To have 10x the power, you must do 100x the useful work. You cannot "buy" the mesh; you must earn it.
- **Consensus Rejection:** If a task (e.g., designing a bioweapon) triggers Consensus Red Flags, the mesh collectively refuses to process the spikes. The actor is left alone with only their local hardware.

## 🛡️ 3. Sovereignty Protections
- **Differential Privacy (DP):** We inject noise into learning updates so no government can "reverse-engineer" your data from the weights.
- **Dark-Node Routing (DNR):** Your IP is hidden behind a rotating session-based **Dark ID**.
- **Metabolic Gating:** Nodes raise their firing threshold when battery is low, ensuring the mesh never "kills" its host.

**Summary:** We aren't building an AI with a "Safety Policy." We are building an Organism with an Immune System.
