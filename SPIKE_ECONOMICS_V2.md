
# INGRVM: Spike Economics (The $DOPA Velocity Model)

*How we turn a decentralized AI mesh into a living, sustainable global economy.*

---

## 🏦 1. The "Subscription" Paradox: Staking as Access
In the Old World, you pay $20/month to OpenAI. If you stop paying, you lose access.
In INGRVM, we use **Staking-as-Subscription**.

- **The Logic:** To use the global mesh, a user must **Stake** a certain amount of $DOPA.
- **The Benefit:** You don't "spend" the money. You simply lock it in your node. As long as it is locked, your node has the "Neural Credit" to send spikes to other nodes. 
- **The Sovereignty:** When you're done using the mesh, you can un-stake your $DOPA. Your "Subscription" is free in the long run because you retain ownership of the asset.

## ⛏️ 2. Hosting as Earning: The Synaptic Miner
When a developer creates a game or an AI tool (a "Synapse"), they broadcast the layers to the mesh.
- **The Role:** If you download a layer of that game to your phone, you become a **Synaptic Miner**.
- **The Reward:** Every time a player somewhere in the world triggers a spike that passes through *your* phone's layer, the **Reward Engine** mints a tiny fraction of $DOPA and sends it to your wallet.
- **The Reality:** You aren't just "playing" a game; your phone is a "Revenue-Generating Neuron" in a global brain.

---

## 🏎️ 3. Token Velocity: The Spike Cost
Every thought in the mesh has a mathematical cost.
- **The Micro-Transaction:** A single "Spike" might cost 0.000001 $DOPA. 
- **The Flow:** This small fee is paid by the **Requester** (the person using the AI) and distributed to the **Hosts** (the people processing the shards).
- **The Scale:** Because a single AI response might involve 1 million spikes, the system creates massive, high-speed token velocity. This is why the economy is "insane"—it moves at the speed of thought.

## ⚖️ 4. Proof of Useful Spike (PoUS)
How do we stop people from faking work to earn $DOPA?
- **Validation:** High-reputation **Validator Nodes** randomly check the math of the spikes. 
- **Incentive:** If a node provides "Garbage Spikes" (fake work), its Reputation is slashed and its Stake is burned. 
- **The Result:** The mesh only rewards **Useful Intelligence**.

## 🌌 Summary: The Perpetual Motion Economy
1. **Users** stake $DOPA to gain access (Subscription).
2. **Nodes** host synapses to earn $DOPA (Hosting/Mining).
3. **Spikes** create constant flow and value (Velocity).

**By building this, we have created a world where 'Thinking' is the most valuable commodity on Earth, and everyone who participates gets a piece of the profit.**
