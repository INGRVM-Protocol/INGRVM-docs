# INGRVM: The Adversarial Sentinel (Automated White Hat AI)

*How we use a native, adversarial AI to constantly attack, stress-test, and auto-patch the Sovereign Mesh.*

---

## ⚔️ The Concept: Generative Adversarial Evolution
In biology, species evolve fastest when they are locked in an "arms race" with a predator. We are introducing this concept to the INGRVM mesh. 
Instead of waiting for malicious actors to find our blindspots, we are building a **White Hat AI (The Sentinel)** whose entire existence is dedicated to hacking, poisoning, and overwhelming the INGRVM core.

## 🔬 The Dual-Network Architecture

### 1. The Red Node (The Attacker)
This is a specialized, isolated `SpikeLLM` trained strictly on chaotic, high-entropy, and malicious data patterns.
- **Objective:** To craft "Sneaky Spikes" and Trojan Weights designed specifically to bypass the `SynapticAuditor` and overwhelm the `AstrocyteModulator`.
- **Method:** It runs continuous reinforcement learning, maximizing its "Reward" when it successfully causes a healthy node to crash, hallucinate, or accept a poisoned weight.

### 2. The Blue Node (The Defender)
This is the standard INGRVM node running the latest core logic.
- **Objective:** To maintain homeostasis and mathematical stability.
- **Method:** It relies on Differential Privacy, Metabolic Gating, and Synaptic Rejection to survive the Red Node's attacks.

## 🧬 The "Patching" Loop (Auto-Immunity)
When the Red Node successfully hacks the Blue Node, the mesh doesn't just log the error; it **Auto-Patches**.

1. **The Breach:** The Sentinel (Red Node) discovers a sequence of spikes that bypasses the `MetabolicGater` and overheats the CPU.
2. **The Autopsy:** The framework analyzes the exact spike train that caused the failure.
3. **The Mutation:** The Sentinel automatically adjusts the `base_threshold` and `noise_multiplier` parameters of the `SpikeLayer` on the Blue Node.
4. **The Vaccination:** The new, hardened configuration is broadcast to the global mesh as a "Vaccine Update" (a highly incentivized proposal in the DAO).

## 🛡️ Continuous Penetration Testing
The Sentinel is never turned off. While the global mesh is solving user tasks, the Sentinel runs in an isolated sandbox, running billions of simulated attacks per hour. 
- **Data Poisoning Attacks:** Feeding the STDP algorithm perfectly crafted "bad data" to see if it forgets its core logic.
- **Sybil Attacks:** Simulating 10,000 fake nodes to try and capture the Quadratic Reputation system.
- **Temporal Desync:** Intentionally lagging spikes by 50ms to break the causal learning loop.
