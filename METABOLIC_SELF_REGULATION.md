
# INGRVM: Metabolic Self-Regulation (Preventing Heat Death)

*How the mesh prevents itself from consuming too much energy, data, and storage as it scales to billions of users.*

---

## 🛑 The "Big AI" Trap: Constant Overheating
Standard AI (GPUs) is "Always On." Even if you ask a simple question, every transistor in the chip fires. As more people use it, the power grid collapses. 

## 🧠 The INGRVM Solution: The Biological Metabolism
In a real brain, if you run a marathon, your body doesn't send 100% power to your brain; it diverts energy to your legs. The brain has a "Metabolic Budget." We have implemented this same logic into the INGRVM mesh to ensure it never becomes a "Power Vampire."

---

## 🔋 1. Predictive Gating (Surprise-Only Processing)
As we implemented in **Level 5**, the nodes don't fire unless they are **Surprised**. 
- **Scalability Win:** As the mesh grows and "learns" more about the world, it becomes **more silent**, not louder. A mature INGRVM mesh covering the whole planet would actually use *less* energy than a young, "curious" mesh because most data becomes "expected" and thus doesn't trigger spikes.

## 📉 2. Dynamic Firing Thresholds (Energy-Aware Neurons)
We have added a **Metabolic Gating** system. 
- **How it works:** Every node monitors its own "Resource Pressure" (Battery %, Data Cap, CPU Heat).
- **The Reaction:** If your phone battery drops below 20%, the node doesn't just turn off; it **raises its neural threshold**. 
- **The Result:** It becomes "harder to bother" the node. It will only fire for extremely high-priority "Emergency Spikes" and stay silent for low-priority background tasks. The network "breaths" with the available energy of the planet.

## 🕸️ 3. Structural Decay (Storage Homeostasis)
Storage is finite. We cannot save every weight update forever.
- **Synaptic Recycling:** Using **Structural Plasticity**, the mesh identifies synapses that haven't fired in 30 days. These are "De-materialized" (deleted), and that storage space is recycled for new learning. 
- **HDC Compression:** Old memories are compressed from 10,000 dimensions down to 1,000 dimensions—they become "vague instincts" rather than "sharp facts," saving 90% of storage while keeping the "core lesson" alive.

## 💸 4. The Economic Brake ($DOPA Cost)
Every spike has a mathematical cost.
- If a user tries to "Spam" the network, their **Reputation Score** drops, and the cost in $DOPA to fire a spike increases. 
- The mesh uses **Economic Homeostasis** to make "being loud" expensive and "being quiet/efficient" profitable.

---

## 🛡️ The "Ghost" State
When the mesh is saturated, nodes enter a **"Ghost State."** They perform inference using **Passive Reflection** (calculating the answer but not broadcasting it) until a high-reward task appears. This keeps the global "Noise Floor" of the internet at near-zero.
