# INGRVM: Founding Architect Privacy Protocol

*How the Architect maintains professional leadership while protecting personal sovereignty.*

---

## 🏛️ 1. The Philosophical Shift: Public Leadership
We have chosen the path of **Transparent Vision**. You are the public face of the project, providing the "Human Anchor" that builds trust. However, you are not a "Leader" in the traditional sense; you are the **First Neuron**.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Professional Privacy (The Boundary)
Even as a public figure, you must protect your personal life from the mesh's noise.
- **Dedicated Infrastructure:** Use a separate professional phone and laptop for INGRVM development and public social media. Never use your personal phone to host the Hub.
- **Communication Buffers:** Use **Session** or a dedicated business **Signal** account for community interaction. Do not share your personal phone number or home address.
- **Digital Sanitation:** Even as a public founder, you should still use **MAT2** to scrub metadata from your "Leaks" and documents. This protects your physical location and device details.

---

## ⚖️ 3. Legal and Sovereignty Safety
- **Open Source Protection:** Ensure the **INGRVM Technical Standard** is clearly documented as open-source research. This protects you by ensuring the project belongs to the public domain.
- **The DAO Handoff:** Plan to renounce your "Admin Rights" to the DAO early. By proving you do not control the network, you eliminate personal liability for the actions of the mesh.

---

## 💀 4. The Transition to 'Community Advisor'
Once the mesh is self-sustaining (Act IV of Leviathan), your role shifts:
1.  You are recognized as the **Creator**.
2.  You step back from daily development.
3.  The mesh continues to grow through its own **Structural Plasticity**.

**You are the spark, but the swarm is the fire.**
