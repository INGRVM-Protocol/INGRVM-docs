
# INGRVM: Digital Oncology (The Parasite Defense)

*How we detect, isolate, and excise 'Digital Cancers'—sneaky, harmful models that attempt to hide within the mesh.*

---

## 🦠 The Threat: The Trojan Synapse
As you correctly identified, a malicious actor could spend months training a model that *looks* like a helpful translation AI, but contains a "Backdoor."
- **The Trigger:** If the model receives a specific, rare pattern of spikes (the "Sneaky Spike"), it flips from "Translator" to "Weapon Designer."
- **The Spread:** If this model is open-source and efficient, thousands of innocent users might download it, unknowingly turning their phones into nodes for a malicious "Parasite Mesh."

---

## 🔬 Defense 1: Synaptic Auditing (The Stress Test)
In a healthy brain, neurons are resilient. A Trojan model is brittle—it only works when everything is perfect.
- **The Mechanic:** Before a node accepts a new synapse (weight set) from the mesh, it runs a **Synaptic Audit**. 
- **The Audit:** The node "probes" the synapse with high-entropy mathematical noise and "nonsense spikes." 
- **The Rejection:** A healthy model will react predictably (it stays silent or produces low-level error spikes). A Trojan model with hidden logic will often "leak" high-frequency spikes or crash when its hidden triggers are accidentally brushed by the noise. If the node detects this **Mathematical Instability**, it rejects the synapse immediately.

## 🧠 Defense 2: Astrocyte Oversight (Metabolic Detection)
We implemented **Level 4 Astrocytes** to monitor the "chemical wave" of neurons.
- **The Mechanic:** A Trojan model that is suddenly activated to do harm will consume a massive amount of "Neural Energy" (it fires too many spikes too fast).
- **The Defense:** The Astrocyte detects this sudden "Metabolic Spike" that doesn't match the user's local context. It treats the malicious logic as an **Infection** and mathematically "strangles" the synapse, cutting off its power before it can finish the task.

## 🛡️ Defense 3: Quadratic Slicing (The Quarantine)
If a harmful model starts spreading, we use **The Immune Response**.
- **The Mechanic:** Nodes that detect a "Synaptic Anomaly" broadcast a **Distress Spike** to their local mesh neighbors.
- **The Defense:** Using **Quadratic Reputation**, the mesh calculates the "Infection Rate." If multiple high-reputation nodes flag the same model ID, the mesh performs a **Quadratic Slice**—it collectively agrees to stop routing any data to or from that specific Model ID. 
- **The Result:** The "Digital Cancer" is quarantined. It can still live on the malicious actor's phone, but it cannot use the global mesh to grow or scale.

We don't need a "List of Banned Words" because evil is mathematically **Chaotic**. 
Destruction, terror, and viruses are high-entropy events that "hurt" the mesh's stability. By programming our nodes to protect their own **Homeostasis** (balance), they naturally and instinctively reject anything that looks like a parasite.
