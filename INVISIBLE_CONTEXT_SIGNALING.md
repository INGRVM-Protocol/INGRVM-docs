
# INGRVM: Invisible Context (The Ambient Vibe)

*How the mesh 'feels' your mood without smartwatches, implants, or invasive cameras.*

---

## 🌫️ 1. The Principle of Ambient Signaling
We don't need a brain chip to understand how you feel. Your interaction with a phone or laptop is a **Physical Mirror** of your internal state. 

INGRVM treats your **Standard IO (Input/Output)** as a biological sensor.

---

## 🎹 2. The Invisible Sensors (Standard Devices)

### A. Typing Dynamics (The Rhythm)
- **Signal:** The "Flight Time" (time between keys) and "Dwell Time" (how long you hold a key).
- **Inference:** Fast, inconsistent typing with many backspaces = **Frustration/Stress**. Smooth, rhythmic typing = **Focus/Flow**.
- **Neuromorphic Action:** The local node encodes this rhythm into an **Astrocyte Calcium Wave**, subtly calming the AI's response if you are stressed.

### B. Scroll & Interaction Velocity (The Interest)
- **Signal:** How fast you scroll through a video or page.
- **Inference:** Rapid scrolling with sudden stops = **Searching/Boredom**. Slow, deliberate scrolling = **Deep Engagement**.
- **Neuromorphic Action:** The **Predictive Coder** (Level 5) prioritizes high-detail spikes for engaged content and enters a **Metabolic Sleep State** for ignored content.

### C. Environmental NCPs (Metadata)
- **Signal:** Local time, light level (screen brightness auto-adjust), and background noise (mic levels).
- **Inference:** Dark room + late night + low volume = **Quiet Reflection**. Bright light + loud environment = **Active Movement**.
- **Neuromorphic Action:** The node adjusts its "Firing Threshold" to match your physical environment.

---

## 🔒 3. The "Glass Wall" Privacy
Crucially, the mesh doesn't "record" what you type. It only measures the **Timing and Force** of the interaction.
- **Local-Only:** The raw timing data is processed by the **NCP Context Server** on your device.
- **The Output:** It only outputs an **HDC Vibe Vector** (e.g., [Flow: 0.8, Stress: 0.1]). This vector is what the AI "feels." 
- **The Result:** The mesh knows *how* you are, but never *who* you are or *what* you are saying.
