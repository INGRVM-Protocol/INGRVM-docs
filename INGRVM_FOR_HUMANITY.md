**The Problem:** Medical data is highly sensitive. Hospitals and individuals cannot legally or safely send their real-time health data to a centralized tech company for AI analysis.
**The INGRVM Solution:**

## 🌾 2. Ending Famine (Neuromorphic Precision Agriculture)
**The Problem:** Deploying standard AI to farms requires expensive hardware, constant 5G connectivity, and massive batteries. 
**The INGRVM Solution:**
- **Zero-Power Edge Nodes:** Because INGRVM uses **Predictive Coding (Level 5)**, a node connected to a soil sensor in a remote field only uses energy when there is a "Surprise" (e.g., a sudden drop in moisture or a pest detection).
- **NCP Servers in the Dirt:** A $5 INGRVM chip running on a coin-cell battery can survive for years in a field, communicating peer-to-peer via LoRa. It tells the farmer exactly which square meter of crops needs water or fertilizer, drastically increasing yield and eliminating waste.

## 🌪️ 3. Halting Climate Change (Grid Optimization & Green AI)
**The Problem:** The transition to renewable energy (solar, wind) is chaotic. The wind doesn't always blow. Power grids need real-time, highly complex optimization to balance supply and demand without relying on fossil-fuel backup generators.
**The INGRVM Solution:**
- **The Living Grid:** A decentralized energy grid requires a decentralized brain. INGRVM nodes installed in solar inverters and home batteries can use **Swarm Intelligence** to negotiate power sharing at the speed of light, balancing the grid neighborhood by neighborhood.
- **The Green Alternative:** Simply by replacing traditional GPU-based AI with INGRVM's neuromorphic architecture, we reduce the carbon footprint of global computation by orders of magnitude.
