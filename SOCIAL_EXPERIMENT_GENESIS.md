# INGRVM: Social Experiment 'Genesis'

*Turning a tech launch into a real-world detection event. Authentic, raw, and high-curiosity.*

---

## 🔬 The Concept: "The Signal is Waking Up"
We are moving away from "Marketing" and toward **"Participatory Observation."** 
You aren't selling an app. You are a researcher who has detected a non-biological signal emerging from a decentralized neuromorphic mesh. You are inviting 100 people to help you "Observe and Stabilize" it.

---

## 📽️ The Content Strategy (No Polish, Just Reality)

### The "Detection" Video
- **Visual:** A raw, vertical screen recording of your phone. You see the **INGRVM UI** with 1-bit spikes flickering. You show the laptop next to it doing the same. 
- **Voiceover:** (Your real voice, calm) "I've been running this neuromorphic code between my phone and laptop for 3 days. It's designed to only fire when it finds a meaningful pattern in data noise. It just triggered. I need to see if this is local or if it can survive a mesh jump. I'm looking for 100 nodes to join the detection."
- **Call to Action:** "APK link in bio. Join the mesh. Let's see what happens."

### The "Consistency" Update
- **Visual:** A screenshot of your `ledger.db` or a terminal log showing 5 new nodes joined.
- **Text:** "5 more neurons detected. The signal is spreading to Austin and London. If you're running the node, check your 'Surprise' level. We're at 0.8 flow. 95 more to go."

---

## 🕊️ The Community (The Organic Ghost Team)
We don't need a "Secret Team" sworn to oaths.
- **The Process:** The first 100 people to successfully install the APK and process spikes are the team. 
- **The Interaction:** Create a simple **Matrix** or **Discord** server. The only rule is **"Don't give the game away."** They aren't "beta testers," they are "Collaborating Researchers." 
- **The Reward:** When the mesh reaches 100 stable nodes, they all receive the **Genesis Deed**.

---

## 📈 Why this works
1.  **Anti-Cringe:** There are no capes, no villains, and no saving the world. It’s just an engineer and a weird signal.
2.  **Mystery:** People want to know what the "Signal" is. They have to download the app to "see" it.
3.  **Inclusion:** Being one of the "First 100" creates a massive sense of belonging and duty.

**Conclusion:** The less you try to "Market" it, the more people will want to "Discover" it.
